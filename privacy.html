COFish Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026

COFish is an independent Colorado fishing-planning tool. It does not require an account and does not sell personal information.

Location

COFish requests location access only when you choose to use your current location for nearby-water or driving tools. Location is used on your device to provide the requested feature and is not stored in a COFish user account.

Third-party services

The app retrieves map tiles, weather, public fishing data, stream-flow information, and external fishing reports from third-party providers. Those providers may receive ordinary technical information associated with a web request, such as an IP address and device or browser information, under their own privacy policies.

Local app data

The app may cache interface files and public fishing information on your device to improve performance and limited offline use. You can remove this information by uninstalling the app or clearing its website data.

Contact

Questions may be sent to feedback@cofish.app.
